Privacy Policy & Fair-Play Agreement

1. Privacy & Data Protection

We respect your privacy. This application does not track, collect, or sell personal identifying information. There are no advertising trackers, third-party beacons, or telemetry cookies.

WebSocket connections use anonymous ephemeral session IDs that are destroyed immediately upon disconnection. No API keys or private credentials are stored in client code.

2. Fair-Play & Anti-Cheat Regulations

To preserve authentic 1v1 human competition, the following are strictly prohibited:

  • Chess Engines: Use of external engines (Stockfish, Leela Chess Zero) is forbidden.
  • Automated Scripts & Bot Macros: Injected scripts or synthetic events are rejected by hardware event validation.
  • Console & Developer Tool Tampering: F12 tools and console tampering are restricted during live matches.
  • Continuous Timing Delay Bots: Moves executed with artificial fixed-delay signatures are statistically detected.

3. Pre-Move & Tournament Rules

Enjoy fast, responsive gameplay just like Chess.com:

  • Pre-moves: You can queue a move while your opponent is thinking. It will execute instantly with 0ms delay if legal when your turn arrives.
  • Cancel Pre-move: Right-click or click elsewhere to cancel an active pre-move.
  • 10-Minute Clocks: Authoritative server timers govern match durations.
